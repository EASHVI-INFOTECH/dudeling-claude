# Dudeling 1.0.0-rc.5

A whiteboard tutor inside Claude Desktop, for Apple-silicon Macs, in English.

- Lessons drawn and narrated in sync, one part at a time, with a check question.
- Talk or type on the board to ask, answer or interrupt. While Claude is listening, no Enter is needed.
- The voice and speech recognition run on your Mac. No account or key.
- Lessons come back where you left off, for 30 days.

Known limits: Claude's turn ends after a number of exchanges or about 3 minutes of silence (press
"Continue in the chat", then Enter); no Windows, Intel Mac, web, mobile or Cowork; the bundle is unsigned.

Verify the download: `shasum -a 256 -c dudeling-darwin-arm64.mcpb.sha256`
